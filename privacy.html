Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

VideoBot ("the App") is a personal tool used by its operator to create and publish short-form videos to the operator's own connected social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook).

Information we access

When you connect a social media account, the App receives an authorization token from that platform. This token is used solely to upload videos to that same account on your behalf. The App does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data from other users, viewers, or third parties.

How information is used

Authorization tokens are used only to publish content to the accounts you have explicitly connected. They are not used for any other purpose.

Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are not transmitted to any third party other than the official social media platform APIs they belong to.

Data retention and deletion

You can revoke the App's access at any time from the connected platform's settings, which invalidates the stored token. Local tokens can be deleted by removing them from the operator's machine.

Third-party platforms

Use of each connected platform is also governed by that platform's own privacy policy (Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta/Instagram, Meta/Facebook).
